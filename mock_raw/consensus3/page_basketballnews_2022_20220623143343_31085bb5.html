2022 NBA Mock Draft
43
Ryan
Rollins
G - Toledo
Height: 6'3"
Weight: 179
Wingspan: 6'9.75"
Class: Sophomore
Draft day age: 20
Nationality: USA
Projected team: Clippers - #43
Roles: Scorer, Facilitator, Slasher
BOTTOM LINE

Rollins averaged 18.9 points, 6.0 rebounds, and 3.6 assists in his sophomore campaign at Toledo. There are improvements to be made as a shooter, but he excels off the dribble and can put pressure on the rim. With good size and playmaking, and a strong showing at the NBA Combine, he enters the League seeking to maximize the offensive potential.
