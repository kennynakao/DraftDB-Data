NBA mock draft 2023: Phoenix Suns go big in 2023 NBA draft projections

Jeremy Cluff, Arizona Republic
Tue, Jun 20, 2023, 5:55 PM·3 min read

The Phoenix Suns don't currently have a pick in the first round of the 2023 NBA Draft on Thursday, but they are expected to have a selection in Friday's second round.

Who could they take with that selection?

Take a look at some recent NBA mock draft projections for the Suns' second-round pick, which is currently the No. 52 overall pick in the draft (the pick could change depending on the final outcome of the team's trade with the Wizards for Bradley Beal, with Washington currently holding the No. 57 pick in the draft).

With their salary cap limitations after the Beal trade, the Suns could be looking to land an experienced college player who could quickly contribute and provide some depth for the organization behind Devin Booker, Kevin Durant and Beal at an inexpensive salary.

Several 2023 NBA draft projections have the Suns taking a center or power forward with their pick.

Could one of these players end up being their selection?

NBA draft 2023: How to watch, draft order, odds and what Phoenix Suns fans should know

Yahoo Sports: Suns select Connecticut center Adama Sanogo in second round

Krysten Peek writes: "With the addition of Bradley Beal and an established backcourt, the Suns can add some more size in the post and defensive presence this late in the draft with Sanogo. He was one of the best rebounders during the regular season and in the NBA Draft Combine scrimmages and can be slotted beside Deandre Ayton in the post or give solid secondary minutes off the bench right away."

 
Advertisement

NBA Draft Room: Suns pick Kentucky center Oscar Tshiebwe with second round selection

It writes: "Oscar is a force as a rebounder but doesn’t bring much on the offensive end."

More: Draymond Green next team speculation, rumors, odds, landing spots include Phoenix Suns

CBS Sports: Suns land Florida power forward Colin Castleton with second round pick

Kyle Boone writes: "Given how thin the draft is on bigs, Castleton should be seen as a nice second-round value here for the Suns. He averaged 3.0 blocks per game at Florida last season and had a 9.2% block rate that rated top-20 among all qualifying college basketball players, per KenPom data."

 
Advertisement

Bleacher Report: Suns choose Washington center Mouhamed Gueye in second round

Jonathan Wasserman writes: "In a draft with limited true bigs, Gueye should hear his name called after a productive season that highlighted a combination of post skill, mid-range touch, face-up driving potential and rebounding motor."

More: Bradley Beal trade grades: NBA writer explains harsh mark for Phoenix Suns

Walter Football: Suns draft Arizona State forward Marcus Bagley in second round

Mike Elworth writes: "Bagley is a very talented forward who only played 16 games over three seasons. This is a second-round gamble for Phoenix, as the team needs cheap bench help."

 
Advertisement

Heavy.com: Suns go with Tennessee forward Julian Phillips with second round pick

Sean Deveney writes: "Aggressive offensive wing who already has plenty of defensive polish."

More: Bradley Beal trade shifts Phoenix Suns' NBA Championship odds for 2023-24 season

The Ringer: Suns end up with Eastern Michigan wing Emoni Bates in second round

Kevin O'Connor writes: "Finding quality mentors for Bates will be key to his future success. Kevin Durant has a relationship with him, so the Suns feel like a good situation for him to learn positive habits."
