20
Nikola
Jović
F - Mega Basket
Height: 6'10"
Weight: 205
Wingspan: 7'1"
Class: International - 2003
Draft day age: 19
Nationality: Serbia, UK
Projected team: Spurs - #20
Roles: Scorer, Crafty Finisher, Wing Initiator
BOTTOM LINE

Turning only 19 years old two weeks before the 2022 NBA Draft, Nikola Jović is young, skilled and filled with promise. At 6-foot-10, Jović excels as a playmaker and scorer, is comfortable as a ball-handler and is a functional passer. He lacks elite explosiveness and can be a bit streaky as a shooter from deep; however, his confidence and skill set make him a top NBA prospect. He is one to follow closely this season.
